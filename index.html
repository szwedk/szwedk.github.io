THE PATH

Campus to the field.

Five years from a high-school engineering itch to commissioning humanoids in the field.

  1. 2020

    Kellenberg Memorial

    Where it started

    Long Island, New York. Came back years later with a G1 humanoid in tow, to show the next class what robotics looks like now.

  2. 2020–24

    Sacred Heart University

    B.S. Computer Science

    Minors in Cybersecurity and Mathematics. Built IntelliPARK, a campus-wide smart parking platform running computer-vision cameras, ultrasonic occupancy sensors, and edge processing nodes.

  3. 2024

    Unitree-RoboStore

    Integration Specialist

    Bridging engineering, software, and innovation for classrooms, labs, and industry. Designed a line of custom 3D-printed hardware for Unitree platforms.

  4. Now

    Unitree-RoboStore

    Head of Technology

    Humanoid commissioning, VR teleoperation, autonomous patrol. Nights and weekends go to websites, apps, weddings, and whatever else lands on the bench.
