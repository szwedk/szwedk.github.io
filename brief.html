Tell me what
we’re making.

Five questions, about two minutes. At the end it composes the brief as an email for you to send, so my first reply can be useful instead of a list of questions back.

Useful things to include:

  • What you’re making. A robot, a site, an app, a wedding, a product launch, or something that does not have a name yet.
  • When it has to exist. A real date beats “soon”, even if the date moves later.
  • What already exists. Sketches, a repo, a half-built prototype, a brand book, or nothing at all.
  • How you want to work. One deliverable and out, or something I stay on and support.
